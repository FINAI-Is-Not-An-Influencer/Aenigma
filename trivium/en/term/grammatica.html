Grammar

trv:grammatica

IRI https://aenigma.criptasemantica.it/trivium#grammatica

Starting from the vast Hellenistic rhizome of the grammatikē technē, the total art of reading, exegesis, care of texts and understanding of language, the category 'grammar' actualises the field of the 'letter', projected onto the cognitive and technological horizon of our time. Grammar is thus the synthetic womb of all the branches that flowered from that ancient drive to decode the world: the discipline that raises to science writings and arche-writings, texts and hypertexts, tongues and languages, types and characters, signs and communications.

Formalisation of the manifesto

The manifesto begins with perplexity about the word “being”. It connects the question of the sense of being to a history of philosophy, to hyperwriting and to the dependence of different modes of inquiry. The beginner's guide introduces the project through an example.

How to read the formalisation

The OWL schema describes the structure of records. A question and a position are disjoint types of research entry. A position's inverse link to its assertions allows navigation in either direction under OWL entailment. OWL 2

The discourse graph represents a relation as an assertion within a position. The notation Arc(r,p,s,k,o) says that arc r in position p connects source concept s to target concept o using relation kind k. Each position has a source passage, a reading mode, a status and a formula. “Paraphrase” attributes a restatement to the text, “interpretive choice” marks a decision in the model, and “contextual extension” points to another public page. An arc is an aid to retrieval. Its parent formula retains the scope and logical structure.

In the formulas, ∀x:D and ∃x:D restrict a variable to a domain. ¬, ∧, ∨, → and ↔︎ mean negation, conjunction, disjunction, implication and equivalence. := defines an abbreviation. ◇ marks possibility, Oₘ a task prescribed by the manifesto, and ⟦…⟧ a mention in the discourse. OInf, OAn and OFen abbreviate computational, analytic and phenomenological ontology. D(T) is a theory's domain and Dᵣ a domain opened with respect to a region. The Italian names in formulas identify the same concepts across all editions. These formulas are textual descriptions and do not specify a possible-world semantics.

Interpretive choices

Genealogy as attributed discourse. F01 distinguishes the addressees' familiar use from the speakers' former confidence. F03 and F07 attribute the chronology and the priority claim about an abstract machine to the manifesto. Turing's paper records receipt and presentation in 1936 and appears in volume 42 of the Proceedings (1936–37). The manifesto leaves his name implicit. Turing's paper

Writing, conveyability and quantification. F08 describes the quantification of writing as a historical and technical process. F19 relates theory, statement, bound variable and admissible value. In F16, ∀c∈D ◇Veicola(p,c) gives the capacity for each content item, while ◇Veicola(p,c₀) concerns one specified item. The first formula leaves simultaneous conveyability open. F21 ranges over analytic ontologies, including object-oriented and hyperobject-oriented variants, regardless of awareness. F25 makes the dependence of the quantification domain on the inquiry explicit.

Two ontological treatments. F26 records the ontological treatment of an entity as a thing. F27 records the ontic treatment of being in relation to an entity whenever being is thematised in an ontology of entities. F28 places inquiry into being as such and the absence of a selected thing within the scope of the manifesto's prescription. F29 treats the being-there of being as a dimension to indicate. F13 uses Dasein for the human pole of access to content.

Nothing and grounding. F30 uses mention brackets for the limiting reference in the passage. dub:nulla identifies that sense. In OWL, owl:Nothing denotes the empty class. F31 expresses the grounding of sense in one direction. X03 gives the ontic support of inquiry in the other direction. The two relations have separate names and carry no transitivity axiom. F32 leaves the inquiry's totality open. Project

The contextual entries X01–X03 concern projection, the four determinations of thrownness and the map of entities. X04 distinguishes the site, the notes and the recurring hypertext. X05 connects the article to the articulation of an opening. The entries remain separately attributable. Project, About, Articles

Example query

This query retrieves who receives sense from fundamental ontology according to the manifesto, along with the reading and source.

PREFIX dub: <https://aenigma.criptasemantica.it/dubium#>

SELECT ?concept ?position ?reading ?passage ?source WHERE {
  ?arc a dub:RelationAssertion ;
    dub:subjectConcept ?concept ;
    dub:relationKind dub:rel-riceveSensoDa ;
    dub:objectConcept dub:ontologiaFondamentale ;
    dub:belongsTo ?position .
  ?position dub:derivedFrom ?passage ; dub:readingMode ?reading .
  ?passage dub:partOfSource ?source .
}

The expected row is dub:ontologiaEnte, dub:F31, dub:Fondamentale, dub:P8 and the manifesto's URL. The catalogue presents the same route for a reader.

Extend and check the graph

Give a new passage a reproducible locator and a new position an identifier, a source, a status and a force. A competing interpretation gets a separate position. SHACL checks whether the records contain the required data. OWL entailment provides structural links. The validity of an interpretation and the historical claims require reading the sources.

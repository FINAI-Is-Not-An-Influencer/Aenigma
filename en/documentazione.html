Map of the Aenigma documentation

Italiano · Latine

The documentation of Aenigma lives in three places that refer to one another. This site is the reference: for every module it offers the catalogue, the hierarchy, a beginner's guide, the formalisation and a page for every term, in Italian, English and Latin. The repository keeps the graphs, the constraints, the tools, the tests and the changelog. The Wiki gathers the encyclopaedic chapters, the step-by-step exercises and the procedures. None of the three is enough on its own; together they form a single work, designed so that the work of encoding is also an experience of study.

Four reading paths

If you are… Start from Then
New to ontologies the Dubium guide, which starts from an example and reaches a first query Wiki chapter 01 and the exercise of chapter 03
A user of the data (developers, editors, services) the "Files for reuse" of each catalogue: Turtle, JSON-LD, RDF/XML, context, SHACL chapters 05 and 06 on querying and identifiers, the procedures of chapter 15
A scholar (ontology, semiotics, scientometrics) the formalisations of Dubium, Trivium and Metrum chapters 02, 04, 10–13 on the model, the map of the manifesto and the bridge between ontology and scientometrics
A philosopher the Cripta Semantica manifesto and the entry F28, where being is thematised without a chosen thing chapter 14 on the names of Aenigma, Dubium, Trivium and Metrum and chapter 16 on the open questions

How to verify by yourself

Whoever clones the repository can reproduce every check offline: python3 tests/verifica.py tests the whole network (parsing with two parsers, headers, cross-module references, SHACL with eleven alterations, OWL RL inferences); python3 dubium/tests/verifica.py tests Dubium; python3 tools/export.py, python3 tools/build_site.py and bash minify.sh regenerate exports and site; python3 tools/check_site.py resolves every link and anchor. If the site you read and the site you generate coincide, the documentation is that of the graph.
